Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: We only set tracking cookies after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you decline, no tracking cookies are set. Your choice is remembered in your browser for future visits.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences over time. Under the EU ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR, websites must obtain your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

2. How we manage consent

We use Google Consent Mode v2 to manage your cookie preferences. When you first visit our site:

  • All consent types default to denied. No tracking cookies are set.
  • Google Analytics may still receive cookieless pings — these are anonymous, cannot identify you, and are used only to understand basic traffic patterns.
  • A cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen, giving you the choice to Accept or Decline.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key ancya-cookie. On your next visit, we restore your choice automatically — the banner is not shown again.

3. Cookies we use

The table below lists every cookie that may be set on your device, but only after you click "Accept":

Cookie name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors using a random client ID. 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Maintains session state for the GA4 measurement ID G-FLMXGH7WHJ. 2 years

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or third-party social media tracking cookies.

4. Consent types

Google Consent Mode v2 tracks four consent types. When you accept, all four are set to "granted":

  • analytics_storage — allows Google Analytics to set cookies for usage measurement.
  • ad_storage — allows advertising-related storage. (We do not run ads, but this flag is included for Consent Mode v2 compliance.)
  • ad_user_data — allows sending user data to Google for advertising. (Not used; included for compliance.)
  • ad_personalization — allows personalized advertising. (Not used; included for compliance.)

When you decline, all remain "denied" and no cookies are set.

5. How to change or withdraw consent

You can change your consent choice at any time:

  • Clear the stored preference: open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage → ancyatech.com, and delete the ancya-cookie entry. On your next visit, the banner will reappear.
  • Clear all cookies: use your browser's settings to clear cookies and site data for ancyatech.com and .google-analytics.com.
  • Use browser controls: most browsers let you block or delete cookies in Settings → Privacy & Security.

6. Local storage

In addition to cookies, we use the browser's local storage to remember your consent choice (key: ancya-cookie, value: granted or denied). Local storage is not a cookie and does not contain personally identifiable information.

7. Third-party processors

Google Analytics 4 is operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes analytics data as a data processor under GDPR-compliant terms. See Google's privacy and compliance information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our cookie usage changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. For general data protection information, see our Privacy Policy.
