The design phase

Start from the decision, not the template.

Before a single pixel is placed, we map what a visitor needs to decide: what you sell, who it is for, what it costs, and what happens next. The layout grows out of that sequence.

You review real screens at every breakpoint — not a mood board. Once approved, the same system becomes the build: components, type styles and spacing rules that stay consistent as the site grows.

  • Wireframes and a clickable flow before visual design
  • A component library you can extend yourself
  • Accessibility and performance checked in the design, not after

Questions

Before you book a call.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?
Yes. Pages are built from documented components, and you get a walkthrough plus the source files. If you prefer a CMS, the Growth plan includes a blog and page editor.
Do you design in Figma?
Design happens in the browser wherever possible, so what you approve is what ships. Static files and component notes are provided for your records.
What if I only need a design, not a build?
That works for teams with an in-house developer. The scope and price change, but the design system deliverables stay the same.
