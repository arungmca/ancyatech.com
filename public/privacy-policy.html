Last updated: November 3, 2026

Plain English: We collect only what you voluntarily give us (your name, email, and message via our contact form) and anonymous analytics about how visitors use the site. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

2. What data we collect

We process two categories of personal data:

  • Contact form submissions — when you fill out the contact form on our homepage, we receive your name, email address, and the message you write. We also receive any other information you choose to include in the message body.
  • Analytics data — if you accept cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects aggregated usage statistics (pages visited, session duration, approximate geographic region based on IP). IP addresses are anonymized (anonymize_ip is enabled). If you decline cookies, Google receives only cookieless "consent mode" pings that cannot identify you.

We do not collect or process special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR), such as health, race, religion, or political opinions.

3. Legal basis for processing

Our processing of personal data is based on:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for analytics cookies. You give consent by clicking "Accept" on the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser storage or contacting us.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for processing contact form submissions. We need the information you provide to respond to your enquiry about our services.

4. How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • To understand how visitors use our website and improve its content and performance.
  • To maintain records for accounting and business purposes where required by law.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following processors, all of which are bound by data processing agreements compliant with the GDPR:

  • Google Ireland Limited — provides Google Analytics 4. Google processes analytics data in accordance with its GDPR-compliant data processing terms. Google's privacy and compliance information.

We may also disclose data if required by law, court order, or competent regulatory authority.

6. Data retention

  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months after your last interaction, then deleted unless a longer retention period is required by law.
  • Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for up to 14 months, after which it is automatically aggregated or deleted.

7. International data transfers

Google Analytics processes data within the EU where possible. Any transfers outside the EU/EEA are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, ensuring a level of data protection equivalent to the GDPR.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Article 15) — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Article 16) — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Article 17) — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction (Article 18) — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability (Article 20) — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Article 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Article 7(3)) — withdraw consent for analytics processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email antony@ancyatech.com. We respond within one month, as required by the GDPR.

9. Cookies

We use Google Consent Mode v2 to manage cookies. Tracking cookies are not set until you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you decline, Google Analytics receives only cookieless pings that cannot identify you. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

10. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we do our best to protect your information.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit):
