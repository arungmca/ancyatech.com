Work/Foxtail Café

Case study · Café

A café site that takes orders while the kitchen works.

A menu-first website with online ordering, multilingual content and Google Business sync — rebuilt in eight days.

Client
Foxtail Café
Industry
Hospitality
Services
Design, build, local SEO
Timeline
8 business days

The challenge

A good café hidden behind a bad website.

Foxtail's old site was a template with a PDF menu, no mobile ordering, and a Google profile that listed the wrong opening hours. Weekend staff were spending hours on the phone that should have gone to service.

The brief was simple: make it easy for a customer to see the menu, order, and find the café — on a phone, in the language they prefer.

The approach

One clear action on every screen.

We started from the menu, not the homepage. Every layout was tested against three tasks: order for collection, check allergens, find the café. Content is structured so AI search can read the menu and hours directly.

Built with modern AI tooling, reviewed by hand, and handed over with the code, domain and analytics all in the client's name.
