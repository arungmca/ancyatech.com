The founder

Independent by design. Built with AI. Reviewed by hand.

I'm Antony S. — a freelance web architect and the solo founder of Ancya Tech. I build fast, careful websites and AI automations for small businesses that have outgrown templates but don't want an agency's overhead.

The studio

One person, modern tools, no hand-offs.

Ancya is a one-person studio. You talk to the person doing the work, and the person doing the work answers for it. No account managers, no juniors learning on your budget, no surprise invoices.

I build with modern AI tooling rather than pretending otherwise. It compresses months into days; the architecture, accessibility and performance decisions are still reviewed and owned by me.

Every project ends with the code, assets and accounts in your name. If we ever part ways, nothing stops working.

AI shouldn't be a moat for the companies that can afford it. Used well, it raises the floor for everyone — and small businesses deserve their share of that growth.
