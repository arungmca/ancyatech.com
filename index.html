FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can you launch my website?

Most projects launch in 5–14 business days. We use AI-powered acceleration to compress what traditionally takes two months into two weeks — without cutting corners on design or code quality.

Do I own my website code and assets?

Yes — 100%. You own all source code, vector assets, and credentials from day one. Zero proprietary vendor lock-in. If you ever want to take your site elsewhere, you can.

What is AI SEO / Generative Engine Optimization?

GEO optimizes your website content and structure so that AI search engines like ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Apple Intelligence cite and recommend your business. Traditional SEO gets you ranked on Google; GEO gets you cited by AI.

What AI automations do you build?

We build custom workflows using webhooks, n8n, Make, and custom AI agent scripts. Common automations include lead qualification with instant auto-response, email triage and routing, invoice matching, appointment booking, CRM synchronization, and document processing.

What technology stack do you use?

We build with modern front-end tooling: React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. This gives you 98+ Google PageSpeed scores, sub-second load times, and a maintainable codebase that any developer can work with.

Do you work with businesses outside Belgium?

Absolutely. While we're based in Kortenberg, Belgium, we serve SMBs across the Benelux, DACH region, UK, and internationally. All communication is in English, and we work across time zones.
